# ÇA VA PÉTER! — Product Brief

**Status:** Design baseline from the agreed RÉVOLUTION 2026 PRD v1.0. The original detailed Word PRD remains the source of truth for any elements not restated here. This is not a verbatim import.

## Elevator pitch
A fast, replayable browser arcade game set in a **fictional modern Paris student-demonstration scenario**, featuring two contrasting playable roles, a miniature 3D district and reactive crowd behavior.

## Target experience
- Play in seconds rather than reading a long tutorial.
- Move around a compact, legible Paris-inspired map with a slightly angled top-down camera.
- Choose Student or Civic Safety Officer; each has different, nonviolent objectives.
- Navigate changing routes, safe gathering zones, congestion and public-safety events.
- Finish a roughly three-minute round with a clear result and reason to replay.

## Look and feel
- Expressive, miniature indie-style 3D characters and simplified city architecture.
- Space Grotesk headings; Inter body/UI text.
- Initial proposed palette: Midnight navy #17233A, French blue #3764C9, soft coral #F07162, warm cream #F9E8C9. These values are **new design choices**, not verified samples from Jantar Mantar footage.
- Readable high-contrast HUD and color-independent team distinction.

## MVP scope
- One map; two selectable roles; keyboard controls; basic camera.
- Offline NPCs and three distinct reactive events.
- Three-minute match timer, objective scoring, result screen and restart.
- Basic onboarding, mute and pause controls.

## Later scope
- Invite-based two-team multiplayer, touch controls, spectator and replay features subject to testing.

## Non-goals / guardrails
- No real-world confrontation training, violent mechanics or depictions of identifiable real protesters.
- Not a recreation of an individual protest or a factual representation of the current French movement.
- No use of unlicensed footage, logos, soundtracks or original game assets.

## Acceptance checks for the first playable version
1. A user can start a solo match without an account.
2. Controls respond, camera tracks and collisions prevent walking through buildings.
3. Student and safety roles have clearly different win conditions.
4. Crowd events visibly affect routes and scoring.
5. Match ends deterministically with accurate results, and restart works.
6. Baseline gameplay runs on a modest laptop without a required paid service.
